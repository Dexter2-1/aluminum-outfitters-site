Returns & cancellations

Every order here is custom-built or made to order, so our policy works a little differently than off-the-shelf retail. Here's exactly where you stand:

Before production starts

You can cancel or change your order at no charge any time before we begin fabrication. Reach out by phone or email as soon as possible.

Once production has started

Because materials are cut and welded specifically for your order, deposits become non-refundable once fabrication begins. We'll always confirm with you before starting.

Defects or damage

If your item arrives damaged or doesn't match what was agreed on, contact us within 7 days of delivery and we'll repair, replace, or refund it — your choice.

Stock items

Unused, unmodified stock products (not custom builds) can be returned within 14 days of pickup or delivery for a full refund, minus any shipping cost already incurred.

Questions about a specific order? Call 214 578-4684 or email aluminumoutfittersshop@gmail.com — we're happy to talk through it.
